Academic Biography

I am a hydroclimate scientist interested in how climate variability alters the water cycle and shapes regional water availability and extremes.

My work combines physical reasoning with analysis of large observational, reanalysis, and climate-model datasets. I am especially interested in research questions that connect atmospheric circulation, precipitation, land-surface conditions, and hydroclimate impacts across spatial and temporal scales.

I value reproducible scientific computing, careful interpretation of climate data, and clear communication of complex physical processes.
