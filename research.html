Research Themes

My work sits at the intersection of climate dynamics and hydrology, with an emphasis on understanding variability and extremes in the water cycle using observations, reanalysis, and climate-model simulations.

Theme 01

Hydroclimate Variability

Regional and large-scale variability in precipitation, terrestrial moisture, runoff, and water availability across daily to interannual timescales.

Theme 02

Droughts, Floods & Precipitation Extremes

Physical mechanisms controlling the development, persistence, spatial structure, and predictability of high-impact hydroclimate events.

Theme 03

Climate Dynamics & Moisture Transport

Connections between circulation anomalies, atmospheric moisture transport, land–atmosphere interactions, and regional hydroclimate conditions.

Theme 04

Hydroclimate Data Science

Reproducible analysis of large gridded geophysical datasets using Python, xarray, statistical diagnostics, and scientific-computing workflows.
